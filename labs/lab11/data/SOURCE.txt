MovieLens ml-latest-small (GroupLens Research, University of Minnesota), the version generated on 26 September 2018:
100,836 ratings (0.5 to 5 stars) and 3,683 tags by 610 users for 9,742 films, from 29 March 1996 to 24 September 2018.
https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/latest/
Citation: F. Maxwell Harper and Joseph A. Konstan. 2015. The MovieLens Datasets: History and Context.
ACM Transactions on Interactive Intelligent Systems (TiiS) 5, 4: 19:1-19:19. https://doi.org/10.1145/2827872
Terms: GroupLens usage licence (research and education; the data may not be redistributed without permission;
its use must be acknowledged). This lab therefore ships no MovieLens data: the notebook downloads
https://files.grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/ml-latest-small.zip at run time, or uses a folder ml-latest-small/
next to the notebook.

The notebook keeps the 1,297 films with at least 20 ratings and splits by time at 1 October 2016 (00:00 UTC):
train = ratings before, test = ratings after. Test users: at least 10 ratings and 5 liked films (4 stars or more)
before the cut-off and at least 5 rated films after it (25 users).

llm_prompt.txt: built by labs_instructor/lab11/make_llm_fallback.py from the training period only.
It lists, for 5 test users, the titles of up to 15 films they rated 4 stars or more before the cut-off
(most recent first), and the titles of the 150 films with the most ratings before the cut-off as the candidate list.
The 5 users are drawn (seed 7103) from the test users who have not rated at least 100 of the 150 candidates before the cut-off
and who rated at least 5 candidates after it (6 of the 25 test users), so that an LLM answer can score hits at all.
It holds film titles and MovieLens user numbers only; no ratings, dates or tags.
llm_fallback.txt: the answer of Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (Apache 2.0) to exactly that message, on CPU, bf16,
greedy decoding, max 900 new tokens. Not edited.
