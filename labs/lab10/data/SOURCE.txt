Lab 10 data. Everything here is synthetic, written for this lab by make_data.py (seed 7103); no real
companies, people or job ads. Licence: CC BY 4.0 (COMP7103B, HKU).

job_ads.csv (50 ads) and gold.csv: written from templates with varied wording (structured "Position:" ads, prose
ads, pipe-separated ads; salary per month, as a range, per annum, per hour, negotiable or missing; experience in
digits, words, ranges or "no experience needed"; districts named directly or by a place inside them; a few ads
have an "About us" paragraph, and ads 23 and 41 contain an instruction to AI tools). Company names are invented.
The gold table follows the schema of Lecture 10: title, company, salary (integer, HK$ per month, the lower end of
a range; null for hourly pay or when not stated), district (one of the 18 districts), experience (integer,
minimum years; "no experience needed" = 0), contract (full-time / part-time / contract). Empty cell = not stated.

llm_prompt.txt: the schema prompt with ads 1-5. llm_fallback.txt: the reply of Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct
(Apache 2.0) to exactly that prompt, CPU, greedy decoding. llm_output_45.jsonl: the same model, the same prompt
with ads 6-50 in batches of five; one line per ad: each {...} object of the model's reply, matched to its ad by
the "ad" number the model wrote (or else by its position). The model output is not edited; the raw replies are
kept in the instructor folder (llm_raw.txt).

corpus.csv (210 records): a made-up public library ("HVPL"): 60 help-guide chunks (membership, loans, fees,
rooms, digital services) and 150 catalogue records (title, invented author, year, format, call number, subject,
one-line summary), shuffled. queries.csv: 10 queries with the doc ids judged relevant by the author.
doc_emb.npy (210 x 384) and query_emb.npy (10 x 384): sentence embeddings from
sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (Apache 2.0), normalised to length 1, float32, rows in file order.
