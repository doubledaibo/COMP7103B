Online Retail II: all transactions of a UK-based online retailer (non-store) between 01/12/2009 and 09/12/2011.
The company mainly sells unique all-occasion gift-ware; many of its customers are wholesalers.
Source: Chen, D. (2012). Online Retail II. UCI Machine Learning Repository, id 502.
https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CG6D · Licence: CC BY 4.0.
invoices_uk_2011.csv: sheet 'Year 2010-2011', United Kingdom, invoices dated 2011-06-01 to 2011-11-30.
Removed: cancellations (invoice number starting with C), lines with quantity or price <= 0, lines with no
description, and non-product codes (POST, DOT, M, C2, BANK CHARGES, gift vouchers, ...; kept: 5-digit stock codes
with an optional letter). Each stock code keeps its most common description. Only the 100 products
bought on the most invoices are kept (to keep the file small); invoices with none of them drop out.
Result: 53,628 lines (one per invoice and product), 7,900 invoices, 100 products.
rules_top200.csv: Apriori at support >= 0.75% and confidence >= 5% on these invoices
gives 120,064 rules, 10 of them with lift < 1. The file keeps the 192 rules with the highest
support and the 8 rules with the lowest lift (all below 0.95; negative associations), numbered R001-R200 by lift
(highest first). Support and confidence are fractions (0.030886 means 3.09%).
llm_prompt.txt: the message given to the LLM (the same 200 rules).
llm_summary.txt: the answer of Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (Apache 2.0) to llm_prompt.txt (CPU, greedy decoding),
EDITED FOR THE LAB: the model chose the themes, the wording and most of the citations, but wrote no numbers in them;
we added the numbers in the requested format, completed the cut-off ending, corrected one rule ID, and planted some
errors of the kinds named in Lecture 8. The list of edits is in the instructor's answer key.
